02 / BOUNDARIES

What all three concepts promise

Honest state. Dream, Fabricating, Individually Tested, and Integrated stay distinct. No physical component is presented as integrated.

Safe simulation. Camera, AI, voice, transparency, and diagnostics are labeled samples. Camera passthrough is not walking vision.

Production isolation. The prototype tree is outside the published src\ artifact. Recon Scanner v0.7 is unchanged.

Original local art. Code-drawn visuals are provisional and not fabrication geometry. Purchased armor files were not copied.
