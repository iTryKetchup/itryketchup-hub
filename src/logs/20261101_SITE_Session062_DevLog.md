# Ketchup AR Lab Is Open: Try Recon Scanner v0.7 for Free

**Dev Log #062 — SITE — 2026-11-01**

---

## 1. Ketchup AR Lab Is Open

What started as a simple experiment with AR glasses and a black browser screen has become the first working HUD in the Ketchup AR Lab.

Recon Scanner is an original sci-fi-inspired browser HUD designed for experimentation with AR glasses, tablets, phones, and desktop browsers.

**[Try Recon Scanner v0.7 free](https://www.itryketchup.com/hud/)**

No payment or account is required.

---

## 2. What Is In Recon Scanner v0.7

The current version includes:

- An animated circular radar with occasional simulated green and red contacts.
- A full-screen roaming search scanner.
- An animated compass with simulated headings.
- A live clock, sensor-cycle timer, simulated activity indicators, and measured browser frame rate.
- Automatic transitions between radar and scanner modes.
- Digital screen wipes, adjustable cyan/green intensity, and display-position shifting.
- Optional black-screen breaks and touch-friendly controls.
- A standalone web-app experience for supported devices.

The display uses a true-black background, but its appearance through AR glasses depends on the hardware, lens tint, brightness, and viewing conditions.

---

## 3. Important Safety And Simulation Note

This is a fictional visual experiment. It does not detect objects, hazards, people, or traffic and is not intended for navigation or safety-critical use.

Display-care animations do not guarantee prevention of OLED burn-in.

---

## 4. What Comes Next

Recon Scanner v0.7 is a released experimental version, not the end of development.

Ketchup AR Lab will continue exploring other HUD styles and potentially purpose-built physical helmets, with separate fit and safety testing.

The goal is to make these experiments accessible for other sci-fi and AR enthusiasts to try and enjoy.

This is an independent, unofficial experiment and is not affiliated with entertainment franchises or hardware manufacturers.
